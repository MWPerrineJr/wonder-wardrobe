# Buy a domain through Lovable for email sending

## Goal

Get a domain you own connected to this project so the app can send branded survey emails from your own domain (e.g. `notify@yourdomain.com`).

## Steps

1. **Open Lovable domain purchase**
   - Use the in-app domain purchase flow to search for and buy a domain.
   - The domain is automatically connected to the project after purchase.

2. **Verify domain connection**
   - Confirm the domain appears as Active in Project Settings → Domains.

3. **Set up the email sender domain**
   - Once the domain is active, configure it as the project’s email sender domain.
   - This enables transactional emails like the post-visit survey.

4. **Test email delivery**
   - Run the survey send job and confirm the invite email is delivered to a real inbox.

## Notes

- Domain purchase is available on paid Lovable plans.
- If you prefer not to buy here, you can buy from any external registrar and connect it manually.
