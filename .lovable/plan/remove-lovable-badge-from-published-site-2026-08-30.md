# Remove Lovable Badge from Published Site

## Goal

Hide the "Edit with Lovable" badge that appears in the bottom-right corner of the live site at thestandingchair.com.

## Steps

1. Check current badge visibility setting.
2. Set `hide_badge` to `true` so the badge no longer renders on the published deployment.

## Notes

- Hiding the badge may require a paid Lovable plan. If the setting is blocked, I'll report the reason.
- This only affects the published site; the preview environment may still show builder UI.
